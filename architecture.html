ORYX / ORBITAL VEHICLE

AN ORBITAL STAGE THAT COMES BACK.

D3 combines the functions of an upper stage, orbital spacecraft and returning vehicle.

Traditional upper stages complete their task and are discarded. D3 is designed to continue: reaching orbit, supporting the mission, returning valuable cargo and bringing the flight hardware itself back for inspection and reuse.

ASCENTCompletes the orbital phase of flight after booster separation.
ORBITSupports payload delivery and standalone orbital operations.
RETURNDesigned to return up to 3 t from LEO to Earth.
RECOVERYLands for inspection, servicing and the next mission.

02 / LAUNCHER

A DEDICATED ROUTE TO ORBIT.

For missions that need launch capacity — not a returning spacecraft.

Launcher serves the established mission model: payloads are protected inside a fairing, delivered to the required orbit and deployed while the first stage is recovered for reuse.

PAYLOADUp to 15 t with first-stage reuse.
EXPENDABLE MODEUp to 17 t where the mission requires maximum performance.
MISSIONDedicated conventional payload delivery to orbit.

REUSABILITY

REUSE CHANGES THE SYSTEM, NOT ONLY THE PRICE.

Less replacement

Increasing cadence does not have to require manufacturing an entirely new flight vehicle for every mission.

More evidence

Returning hardware carries physical evidence of real thermal, structural and operational conditions.

Faster learning

Inspection feeds engineering models, the next configuration and the next flight.
