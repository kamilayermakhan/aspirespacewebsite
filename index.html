Q4 2026
ENGINE HOT FIRE

First integrated hot-fire campaign for the aerospike propulsion system validates ignition, steady-state combustion and ground-test data loops.

2027
HOPPER TEST

A vertical hop demonstration verifies low-altitude control, throttle response, landing logic and deployable-leg behaviour for the booster stage.

2028
SECOND STAGE TESTING

D3 flight-article testing advances through integrated systems checks, return-stage handling and standalone demonstration of the upper-stage architecture.

2030
FULL SYSTEM TEST FLIGHT

A full-stack test flight brings R1v5 and D3 together in one mission profile, exercising staging, guidance and recovery choreography across the system.

2031
ORBITAL LAUNCH

The first orbital launch places the system into its intended operating regime and opens the path to reusable cargo transport and routine return capability.
